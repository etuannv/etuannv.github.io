### 💡 What is the Labor Tracking System?

Our application is a comprehensive time and performance tracking platform that seamlessly integrates with ShipStation, designed to monitor and improve your team's workflow in real-time. Whether you're managing 5 or 50 employees, this tool offers clarity, control, and actionable insights—all in one intuitive dashboard.

### 🔧 Key Features

#### ✅ Order-Based Time Tracking

Track how long each worker spends on every order, from the moment they start to final completion. Our system ensures transparency and accuracy in labor data collection.

#### 📈 User Performance Reports

Monitor performance with detailed statistics on daily/weekly output. Quickly identify your top performers and those who need support.

#### ⏱ Real-Time Time Tracking

With our Time Tracker interface, users can start, pause, and complete sessions while automatically logging time entries for timesheets.

#### 📊 Order Completion Analytics

Get a high-level overview of how many orders are completed by each user per day. Perfect for shift reviews, team meetings, or end-of-week wrap-ups.

#### 📅 User Duration Logs

See detailed breakdowns of hours worked per user each day, helping with payroll and ensuring compliance with labor regulations.

### 🎯 Why Choose This System?

- 🔄 **Seamless ShipStation Integration**: Automatically pulls order data from ShipStation and ties it directly to user activity.
- ⚙️ **Customizable Workflow**: Supports multiple stages like Pick → Prep → Clean → Ship, with full history logs per item.
- 💡 **Data-Driven Insights**: Exportable reports for HR, ops, and managers.
- 📤 **CSV Export Capabilities**: Easily analyze or share performance and attendance data with your team.
