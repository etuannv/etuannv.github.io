### 🔧 What Is This Tool?

Our tool automatically collects your Etsy Ads data—both daily and weekly reports—and converts them into downloadable CSV files. It then uploads them to a secure web-based dashboard for easy access, filtering, and exporting.

### ✅ Key Features

#### 1. Automated Data Collection

Forget manual copying. This tool acts like a smart assistant that logs into Etsy, pulls your ad performance data, and saves it for you.

#### 2. Daily & Weekly Reports

View detailed insights on:

- Spent Total and Revenue
- Listings and Departments
- Views, Favorites, Orders, and Ad Conversion Ratios (weekly)
- Class performance

#### 3. Advanced Filtering

Use filters by date range, department, class, listing ID, or revenue/spent range to zoom in on what matters most.

#### 4. CSV Export

Click a button to export any report to CSV. Perfect for backups, sharing with a team, or further analysis in Excel or Google Sheets.

#### 5. Cloud-Based Dashboard

Access your ads data from anywhere. No software installation required. Simply log in and start analyzing.

### 🧠 Why Use This Tool?

- Save Time: No more copying numbers manually.
- Make Better Decisions: Easily see what’s working and what’s wasting your budget.
- Stay Organized: Track thousands of listings across departments and time periods.
- Fully Transparent: All your data is visible, downloadable, and exportable.

### 🖼️ Sneak Peek

Here’s what the tool looks like in action: track daily performance with ad spend and revenue per listing, and analyze weekly trends, conversion rates, and ad effectiveness.

### 🔒 Is My Data Safe?

Yes. Your Etsy credentials are never stored. All scraping happens on a secure server, and your reports are encrypted and accessible only by you.
